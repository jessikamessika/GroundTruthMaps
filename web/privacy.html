Privacy notice: GroundTruth Maps

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Who: Zixuan Zhang, sole proprietor, operating as GroundTruth Studio, jess.jm.zhang@gmail.com.

What I store: your email, how often you bike, the start and end points of routes you save, where you signed up, timestamps and a record of what you agreed to. Your browser also keeps your email and routes in local storage. It also keeps a random ID and a few settings for usage statistics (see below).

Why: to send confirmations and closure alerts.

Who sees it: only me, the founder. Google (Sheets, Apps Script) is my service provider, so data may be stored outside Canada. PostHog (United States) is my service provider for anonymous usage statistics.

How long: 12 months after your last activity, then deleted.

Reports: problem reports and feedback are sent through a Google Form. They may include a note and, only if you choose to give it, a contact email. I review them by hand; they never change the map automatically. Kept up to 12 months; to delete one, use the form below or email me.

Usage statistics: to see how the app is used and improve it, I collect anonymous usage events, such as “route planned”, “sign-up sheet shown”, which route result was shown (for example, clear or rerouted) and how you arrived (for example, a QR code). They don't include your email, your route start or end points, closure names, anything you type, or session recordings. If the app crashes, it may send an error report with the type of error and where in the code it happened, never what you typed, your email or your route. To tell a returning visit from a new one, the app keeps a random ID in your browser's storage. It isn't linked to your email or saved routes. PostHog processes these events on servers in the United States. IP addresses are not stored with usage events. Kept up to 12 months. You can turn this off with the switch at the bottom of this page, which also deletes the random ID. The app also doesn't collect usage statistics if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. The setting is saved in your browser, so clearing site data resets it.

Your rights: unsubscribe via any email's link; see, correct or delete your data with the form below or by email (within 10 business days); complain to the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta. I ask that you please talk to me directly to resolve any problems in an easier way before submitting external complaints.
